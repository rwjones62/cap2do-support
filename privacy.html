Privacy Policy for Cap2Do

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Cap2Do is a personal task and notes app. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

Cap2Do does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What data Cap2Do stores

All tasks, notes, checklist items, and app settings are stored locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage. This data is never sent to us, to Apple, or to any third party. We do not operate any servers that Cap2Do connects to, and the app has no account, sign-in, or sync feature.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Cap2Do schedules local notifications directly on your device using Apple's notification system. No reminder or notification content ever leaves your device.

Analytics and tracking

Cap2Do contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. We have no way of knowing how you use the app, and we do not try to.

Data sharing

Because Cap2Do does not collect any data, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to third parties.

Children's privacy

Cap2Do does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Deleting your data

Since all data lives only on your device, you can remove it at any time by deleting your tasks and notes within the app, or by deleting the app itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
