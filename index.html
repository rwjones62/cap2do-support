Frequently asked questions

How do I add a task?
Open the Today tab and tap the add button to create a new task with a title, due date, and optional reminder.
How do checklist notes work?
Each note can hold a list of items. Tap an item to check it off — completed items move to a "Completed" section with a timestamp showing when you finished them.
Do I need an account?
No. Cap2Do has no sign-in and no account. Everything is stored privately on your device.
Does Cap2Do sync between devices?
Not currently. All data stays local to the device you're using.
How do reminders work?
If you set a reminder on a task, Cap2Do schedules a local notification on your device at that time. Make sure notifications are allowed for Cap2Do in your device's Settings app.
Can I recover a deleted task or note?
Not at this time — deletions are permanent. This is on our list of possible future improvements.

Privacy

Cap2Do collects no personal data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
